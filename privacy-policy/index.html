ShopAI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ShopAI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects information in connection with our AI-powered lead qualification and customer engagement automation project.

ShopAI integrates with Facebook/Meta and uses n8n as an automation workflow platform. When people interact with a Facebook Page connected to ShopAI—for example by commenting on posts—related interaction data may be received and processed to help qualify potential leads and support automated customer responses.

ShopAI is currently operated as a learning and development project. This Privacy Policy is intended to explain our practices clearly and accurately.

Information We Collect

Depending on how ShopAI is configured and how users interact with connected Facebook Pages, we may collect or receive:

  • Facebook Page interaction data, such as comments on posts and related metadata.
  • Publicly available information associated with those interactions, such as a public Facebook profile name or identifier provided through Meta APIs.
  • Content of comments or messages submitted when interacting with a connected Facebook Page, to the extent received by ShopAI.
  • Technical and operational data generated by automation workflows (for example, timestamps and processing status).

Facebook/Meta Data

ShopAI may receive and process Facebook Page interaction data through Meta's developer platform and related APIs. This may include comments on posts and publicly available information associated with those interactions.

Access depends on the permissions granted to the Meta Developer App connected to ShopAI and on Meta's platform policies. Your use of Facebook/Meta products is also governed by Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Receive and process Facebook Page interactions relevant to the connected Page.
  • Classify and qualify potential leads based on interaction content and related context.
  • Support automated customer engagement and responses through configured workflows.
  • Operate, monitor, debug, and improve ShopAI.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to valid privacy requests.

ShopAI does not sell users' personal information.

AI Processing

Interaction data received by ShopAI may be processed by an AI agent to classify or qualify potential leads and to support automated customer responses. AI-generated outputs are used to support the application's intended functionality and may be imperfect.

Data Storage and Retention

Data processed by ShopAI may be stored or handled through the systems and workflows used to operate the application, including automation steps configured in n8n and any related storage destinations set up by the application owner.

We do not currently maintain a fixed, publicly stated retention period for all categories of data. Information is retained only as necessary for the application's intended functionality, for operational and development purposes, or as otherwise configured by the application owner.

Third-Party Services

  • Meta / Facebook: Used to connect to Facebook Pages and receive interaction data through Meta's platform and APIs.
  • n8n: Used as the automation workflow platform that may receive, route, transform, and process data as part of ShopAI workflows.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed by ShopAI against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only with service providers needed to operate ShopAI (including Meta/Facebook and n8n), when required by law, or to protect rights and safety where reasonably necessary.

User Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you that we process.

How to request deletion of your data:

  1. Email privacy@shopai.example with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."
  2. Include enough information for us to locate relevant records where reasonably possible (for example, the Facebook Page involved, approximate date of the interaction, and the public name or other identifier associated with your comment).
  3. We will review your request and, where applicable, delete or de-identify personal information associated with your interactions that we control, subject to any legal or operational limitations.

Information you post publicly on Facebook may also remain on Meta's platform according to Meta's policies. Requests related to data held solely by Meta should be directed to Meta.

Children's Privacy

ShopAI is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
