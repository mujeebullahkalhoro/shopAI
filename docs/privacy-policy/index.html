Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ShopAI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects information in connection with our AI-powered lead qualification and customer engagement automation project.

ShopAI integrates with Facebook/Meta and uses n8n as an automation workflow platform. When people interact with a Facebook Page connected to ShopAI—for example by commenting on posts—related interaction data may be received and processed to help qualify potential leads and support automated customer responses.

ShopAI is currently operated as a learning and development project. This Privacy Policy is intended to explain our practices clearly and accurately. By interacting with a Facebook Page or related services connected to ShopAI, you acknowledge the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Depending on how ShopAI is configured and how users interact with connected Facebook Pages, we may collect or receive the following categories of information:

  • Facebook Page interaction data, such as comments on posts and related metadata associated with those interactions.
  • Publicly available information associated with those interactions, such as a public Facebook profile name, profile identifier, or other information made available through Meta APIs for the connected Page.
  • Content of messages or comments submitted by users when interacting with a connected Facebook Page, to the extent such content is provided through Meta's platform and received by ShopAI.
  • Technical and operational data generated by our automation workflows (for example, timestamps, processing status, and workflow identifiers) needed to run and monitor the application.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information beyond what users choose to share publicly in their Facebook Page interactions or what Meta provides through authorized API access for the connected Page.

Facebook/Meta Data

ShopAI may receive and process Facebook Page interaction data through Meta's developer platform and related APIs. This may include comments on posts and publicly available information associated with those interactions.

Access to such data depends on the permissions granted to the Meta Developer App connected to ShopAI and on Meta's platform policies. We use Facebook/Meta data only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including lead qualification and automated customer engagement related to the connected Page.

Your use of Facebook/Meta products is also governed by Meta's own terms and privacy policies. ShopAI does not control Meta's independent data practices.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Receive and process Facebook Page interactions relevant to the connected Page.
  • Classify and qualify potential leads based on interaction content and related context.
  • Support automated customer engagement and responses through configured workflows.
  • Operate, monitor, debug, and improve ShopAI's automation and AI-assisted features.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to valid requests related to privacy or data management.

ShopAI does not sell users' personal information.

AI Processing

Interaction data received by ShopAI may be processed by an AI agent to classify or qualify potential leads and to support automated customer responses. This processing is performed to help understand the nature of a comment or interaction and to assist with follow-up actions configured in our automation workflows.

AI-generated classifications or suggested responses are used to support the application's intended functionality. Outputs may be imperfect, and the application may be adjusted or reviewed as part of ongoing development.

Data Storage and Retention

Data processed by ShopAI may be stored or handled through the systems and workflows used to operate the application, including automation steps configured in n8n and any related storage destinations set up by the application owner.

We do not currently maintain a fixed, publicly stated retention period for all categories of data. Information is retained only as necessary for the application's intended functionality, for operational and development purposes, or as otherwise configured by the application owner. When data is no longer needed for these purposes, it may be deleted or de-identified according to the application's configuration and available tools.

Third-Party Services

ShopAI relies on third-party services to operate. These currently include:

  • Meta / Facebook: Used to connect to Facebook Pages and receive interaction data (such as comments and related publicly available information) through Meta's platform and APIs. Meta's handling of data is governed by Meta's own policies.
  • n8n: Used as the automation workflow platform that may receive, route, transform, and process data as part of ShopAI's lead qualification and customer engagement workflows. Data handled in n8n is subject to the workflow configuration and n8n's applicable terms and privacy practices for the deployment in use.

These third parties may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies for more information about their practices.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed by ShopAI against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures may include access controls, secure transmission where supported by underlying platforms, and careful configuration of automation workflows.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we work to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. As a learning and development project, security practices may continue to evolve.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share or disclose information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers and platforms that help operate ShopAI, including Meta/Facebook and n8n, as needed to provide the application's functionality.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or integrity of ShopAI, our users, or others, where we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary.
  • In connection with a reorganization, transfer, or similar change related to the project, subject to appropriate safeguards where applicable.

User Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on applicable law and the nature of your interaction with ShopAI, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you that we process.

How to request deletion of your data:

  1. Send an email to privacy@shopai.example with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."
  2. Include enough information for us to locate relevant records where reasonably possible (for example, the Facebook Page involved, approximate date of the interaction, and the public name or other identifier associated with your comment or interaction).
  3. We will review your request and, where applicable, delete or de-identify personal information associated with your interactions that we control, subject to any legal or operational limitations.

Please note that information you post publicly on Facebook may also remain on Meta's platform according to Meta's policies and your Facebook account settings. Requests related to data held solely by Meta should be directed to Meta through the tools and processes Meta provides.

Children's Privacy

ShopAI is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us through a connected Facebook Page interaction, please contact us using the details below so we can take appropriate steps to delete the information where applicable.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or the scope of the ShopAI project. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued interaction with Facebook Pages or services connected to ShopAI after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised Privacy Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
